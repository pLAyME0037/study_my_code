Loose coupling is used in software development to:

✅ Improve maintainability: Changes in one component have minimal impact on others.
✅ Enhance testability: Components can be tested in isolation using mocks or stubs.
✅ Increase flexibility and reusability: Components can be swapped or reused across different systems.
✅ Enable independent scaling and deployment: Crucial in microservices and distributed systems.
✅ Simplify debugging: Issues are isolated to specific modules.
While tight coupling may offer performance benefits in small, stable systems, loose coupling is preferred for scalable, evolving applications.
